# Set reminders and notifications

Reminders help you avoid missed payments, follow-ups, and late actions. They are especially useful when you are managing many cheques and deadlines.

## How to set a reminder

1. Open the cheque or task you want to track.
2. Find the reminder or notification option.
3. Choose the date and time for the reminder.
4. Save it.

You can set reminders for:

- upcoming cheque due dates
- follow-up calls
- payment confirmations
- payment collections
- urgent tasks

## How reminders help your business

Reminders keep your work organised and reduce stress. They help you:

- remember important deadlines
- follow up on pending work
- avoid missed payments
- stay professional with customers and vendors

## How to manage reminder settings

If the app allows custom reminder settings:

1. Open your profile or settings.
2. Update reminder preferences.
3. Choose how often you want to be reminded.
4. Save the settings.

## Best practices

- Set reminders early, not at the last minute
- Use reminder timing that fits your daily routine
- Review reminders regularly
- Keep urgent items on top of your list

## Common mistakes to avoid

- forgetting to set reminders for deadlines
- relying only on memory for large payment lists
- leaving the same reminder unchanged after a business change

## Quick summary

Reminders are like a helpful assistant for your work. They do not replace your judgment, but they help you stay on time and stay organised.
