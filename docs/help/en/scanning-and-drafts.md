# Scan cheques and use drafts

Cheque Check can save incomplete records as drafts and can optionally help fill a cheque form from a photo. Manual entry is always available.

## Save and resume a draft

Forms for cheques, parties, and accounts can save an unfinished draft automatically. If you leave a changed form, choose **Save draft** to keep it or **Discard** if you no longer need it.

Open **Drafts** to:

- search by name, number, or bank;
- filter by cheque, party, or account;
- sort by last edited;
- reopen a draft;
- delete a draft permanently.

Drafts are incomplete records. They do not count toward the Free active-cheque limit and do not appear as published cheques on normal lists, reports, or totals.

## Start an AI scan

You can use the dashboard **Scan Cheque** action or the photo control on a new cheque.

Before scanning:

- Use one cheque front, not a collage, PDF, or back-only image.
- Use a clear JPEG, PNG, or WebP image up to 10 MB and 16 megapixels.
- Make sure you have available, unexpired AI scan credits.
- Keep AI Scan Mode on in **Features**.
- Read the disclosure explaining that the image is sent to OpenAI for extraction.

Turning AI Scan Mode off still preserves draft and manual image-entry workflows, but no new AI extraction is started.

## Review the result

After processing, the scan sheet guides you through:

1. Reviewing extracted cheque fields and image evidence.
2. Correcting values such as cheque number, amount, date, party, or account.
3. Choosing an existing party or account, or explicitly creating one.
4. Reviewing any duplicate-cheque warning.
5. Returning to the cheque form to publish.

Suggested matches are not automatic approvals. Verify the selected party and account from the available details. AI can make mistakes, particularly with handwriting, low-quality images, names, and dates.

## Closing or recovering a scan

Closing the scan sheet does not cancel accepted work. If an uploaded cheque was saved as a draft, you can reopen the draft and return to the scan. Saved uploaded drafts can be recovered on another signed-in device.

An image selected but not uploaded/saved does not survive browser closure. When the network outcome is uncertain, use the saved-result check before starting a fresh scan.

## Failed or unusable scans

If scanning times out or returns no useful result:

- keep the uploaded draft;
- retry from the same draft when offered;
- retake the image for a clearer cheque front;
- or continue by entering the fields manually.

A failed, cancelled, timed-out, or unusable request should not be treated as a successfully extracted cheque.

## Credits and privacy

Scanning uses separate AI credits, not Premium or voice-call credits. The app reserves credits for accepted work and settles them according to the final scan state. Do not upload multiple cheques or unrelated documents.

Review the privacy disclosure before scanning. The image and extracted financial data are sensitive business information, and provider processing terms are separate from your account preferences.
