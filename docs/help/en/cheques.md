# Manage cheques

A cheque is a recorded payment between a party and a business account. The current cheque workflow supports issued cheques for Free users and received (inward) cheques through Premium, while existing received cheques remain editable.

## Create a cheque

1. Confirm the active business.
2. Open **Cheques** and choose **New Cheque**.
3. Choose **Issued** or **Received** when that option is available.
4. Select or create the party.
5. Select or create the bank account.
6. Enter the six-digit cheque number, cheque date, positive amount, expected deposit date, and optional notes.
7. Add tags if useful.
8. Review the details and save.

For issued cheques, the party is the recipient. For received cheques, the party is the payer.

If you leave an unfinished form, Cheque Check asks whether to save or discard the draft. See [Scan cheques and use drafts](scanning-and-drafts.md).

## Create from a photo

The dashboard and cheque form can open the cheque photo scanner. AI can suggest fields, but it never replaces your final review.

- Use one clear cheque front in JPEG, PNG, or WebP format.
- Maximum upload limits are 10 MB and 16 megapixels.
- Accept the AI/privacy disclosure before processing.
- Review names, dates, cheque number, amount, party, and account before publishing.
- Choose manual entry whenever scanning is unavailable or you do not want to use credits.

## Current statuses

Use:

- **Issued** for an outward cheque that has not cleared.
- **Received** for an inward cheque that has not cleared.
- **Cleared** after the recorded payment outcome is confirmed.
- **Bounced** when the cheque is dishonoured.

List and filter labels may group issued and received records as **Pending**. Cheque Check does not verify a bank's clearance or account balance.

## Update a cheque

Open a cheque, review its audit log if needed, choose **Edit Cheque**, make the correction, and save. Common updates include:

- correcting a name, date, number, amount, account, or note;
- replacing an image;
- changing status to Cleared or Bounced;
- updating tags.

The detail page keeps a chronological audit log of recorded changes.

## Search, sort, and filter

Open **Cheques**, then use search or **Sort & Filter**. Current filters can include:

- status or direction;
- party type, party, tag, or bank account;
- cheque date and due/deposit-date ranges;
- amount ranges;
- newest, oldest, date, or amount sorting.

The filter sheet shows the number of matching cheques while you adjust it. If no records match, the screen explains the search, filter count, and checked business, and offers **Clear search & filters**.

## Duplicate numbers

The app warns when a cheque number already exists for the selected account. If AI scanning produced the warning, review the duplicate before publishing and confirm only when you intentionally need a separate record.

## Premium actions

Premium tools can add:

- duplicate-to-draft;
- bulk CSV/Excel import;
- reusable cheque templates;
- saved filter views;
- reviewed bulk status changes;
- cheque printing with saved layouts;
- party SMS preparation.

Each tool opens a preview or confirmation step. Review the affected records before confirming.

## Delete a cheque

Delete only when the record was created by mistake or is no longer required. Existing account, party, report, audit, and recovery policies still apply. Do not use deletion to represent a bounced or cleared cheque; update the status instead.

## Good habits

- Record cheques promptly.
- Check the active business, party, account, amount, and dates.
- Preserve leading zeros in cheque numbers.
- Update status when the outcome is known.
- Keep old or bounced records for follow-up and reporting unless they are genuinely obsolete.
