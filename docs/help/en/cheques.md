# Manage cheques

A cheque is a payment record. In Cheque Check, each cheque is connected to a party, a bank account, a date, and a payment status.

This is the heart of the app: tracking what is due, what is paid, and what still needs attention.

## How to create a cheque

1. Open the Cheques section.
2. Choose the option to add a new cheque.
3. Select the party related to the cheque.
4. Choose the bank account.
5. Add the cheque number, date, amount, and type.
6. Save the cheque.

### Typical cheque details

- Party name
- Cheque number
- Cheque date
- Amount
- Payment type
- Account used
- Current status

## How to update a cheque

1. Find the cheque in the list.
2. Open its details.
3. Change any field that needs correction.
4. Save the updated record.

This is useful when:

- the cheque date changed
- the amount was entered wrong
- the status changed
- the bank account was different

## How to check a cheque status

Cheques usually move through different stages. Common statuses may include:

- Pending
- Paid
- Returned
- Cancelled
- Cleared

Check the status in the cheque list or detail view so you know what still needs action.

## How to search and filter cheques

Use the filters to quickly focus on what matters.

1. Go to the Cheques section.
2. Use the search box or filters.
3. Filter by date, party, amount, status, or account.
4. Review the matching cheques.

This helps when you need to find a specific payment fast.

## How to group or sort cheques

You can organise cheques by:

- date
- party
- bank account
- payment status
- tag

Sorting helps you review business cash flow more clearly.

## How to mark a cheque as paid or completed

1. Open the cheque details.
2. Find the status field.
3. Change the status to Paid, Cleared, or another final stage.
4. Save the record.

This keeps your record accurate and prevents confusion later.

## How to handle duplicate or incorrect entries

If a cheque record was entered by mistake:

1. Open the entry.
2. Correct the details or update the status.
3. If the entry is no longer needed, remove or archive it carefully.

### Important

Only delete or archive records when you are certain they are not needed for business tracking or reporting.

## Best practices

- Record cheques as soon as they are issued or received
- Check the party and account before saving
- Keep status updates current
- Review the dashboard regularly
- Do not leave old cheques in an unclear status

## Common questions

### Why is the party important on a cheque?

Because the party tells you who the money belongs to or who the payment is for.

### What if I forget to enter a cheque?

It is better to add it as soon as possible. You can still update the record later.

### What is the best way to stay organised?

Keep the status current, use tags when needed, and review the list often.
