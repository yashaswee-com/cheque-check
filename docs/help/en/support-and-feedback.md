# Feedback, roadmap, and support

Use the support tools in the app for a problem report, product suggestion, or help with your account.

## Search help

Open **Settings → Help & Support** to search the in-app FAQs by question and category. Use related questions to move between setup, cheque entry, scanning, plans, notifications, and security.

## Send feedback

1. Open **Feedback**.
2. Choose **Report a bug**, **Request a feature**, or **Other feedback**.
3. Add a clear title and a description of what happened and what you expected.
4. Attach up to two supported files if they help, with a combined maximum of 10 MB.
5. Submit and use **Your submissions** to track status.

Supported attachments are JPEG, PNG, WebP, PDF, or plain text. You can send at most five feedback submissions per hour.

Bug reports and ordinary feedback stay private. Feature requests appear on the roadmap only after the team publishes them.

## What to include

For a problem, include:

- the route or feature you were using;
- the steps that reproduce it;
- what you expected and what happened;
- the time, browser, and device if relevant;
- a safe screenshot or document after removing private information.

Do not include passwords, verification codes, card details, online-banking credentials, complete bank-account numbers, or unnecessary personal data.

## View the roadmap

The signed-in roadmap shows published feature requests and their status. Filtering and searching your own submissions is available from the feedback area.

Submitting a feature request does not guarantee that it will be approved, scheduled, or built.

## Contact support

The Help & Support page offers:

- email at `support@chequecheck.com`;
- WhatsApp support;
- the current in-app FAQ and feedback tools.

For a payment or access problem, include the relevant order or transaction ID, but never payment-card details. For a cheque or account issue, describe the workflow and send a minimally redacted screenshot rather than a full export.

## Announcements and What's New

**Settings → What's New** and **Announcements** show product updates or messages that the app has made available. A checked-in feature or announcement is not proof of hosted deployment for every account.
