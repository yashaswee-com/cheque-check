# Use the cheque bounce and legal notice pages

Cheque Check also provides public tools for organising the next steps after a cheque bounce. These pages are available without signing in.

## Cheque bounce timeline calculator

Open the **Cheque Bounce** page from the public site. The tool asks for:

- the cheque bounce/return date;
- an optional notice-sent date.

It displays an illustrative timeline for:

- the demand-notice deadline;
- the payment window;
- the complaint-filing deadline.

The calculator uses the entered notice date as the starting point for the displayed 15-day payment window. If the notice is actually received on a different date, calculate that timeline separately.

The calculator is a planning aid based on the dates entered. It does not read your cheque records, contact a bank, verify service or receipt of notice, preserve court deadlines, or make a legal determination.

## Mark the cheque in the app

The public calculator does not update a cheque record.

In your signed-in cheque record:

1. Open the cheque.
2. Update the status to **Bounced**.
3. Add an accurate note if useful.
4. Keep the original cheque number, amount, party, date, and account details for follow-up.

Bounced records are excluded from pending totals and can be reviewed separately in reports.

## Legal notice page

The public legal-notice page currently provides a drafting checklist and links back to the calculator and support. It is not a completed document generator and does not file, send, or serve a notice.

Before using a demand notice, verify:

- cheque number, date, bank, amount, and return memo;
- the applicable statutory time calculation;
- the drawer's correct address and service method;
- signed copies and proof of service;
- the correct forum and local procedure.

## Legal limitation

These pages provide general information, not legal advice. Dates and legal consequences can depend on facts, receipt dates, jurisdiction, and current law. Consult a qualified advocate for your situation.

Do not place private cheque images, personal identification, or complete account details in public support forms merely to calculate a date.
