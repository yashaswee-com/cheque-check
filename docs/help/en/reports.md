# Review reports and exports

Reports and exports are Premium tools. They summarise recorded cheque data for the active business and let you download a report when you need to review or share it.

## Review the report page

1. Open **Reports**.
2. Check the selected business.
3. Choose the statistics date range.
4. Review status totals, pending amounts, distribution, and the recent-cheque preview.

The preview shows at most 10 recent cheques. Use the export to include the complete authorised history.

The statistics date filter controls the on-screen summary. It does not silently limit the full export.

## Export formats

Use **Export** and choose:

- **CSV:** complete selected-business data through a background export job.
- **JSON:** complete selected-business data through a background export job.
- **PDF:** the Business Cheque Overview, prepared in the browser.
- **Excel:** a workbook prepared from the authorised export data.

Large CSV or JSON exports can show a queued/running/ready/failed job. Keep working in the app and return to **Background exports** to refresh or download the completed file.

## Business Cheque Overview PDF

The PDF is an owner-focused report for the active business's complete exportable history. It can include:

- pending receivables and payables;
- cleared and bounced values, kept separate;
- today's, next-7-day, next-30-day, and overdue windows;
- parties with the largest pending amounts;
- monthly activity and month-to-month comparisons;
- a paginated cheque register.

The report follows your language, currency, date format, and time zone. It can include names, cheque numbers, bank names, and amounts. Account numbers, contact details, and internal notes are excluded from this overview, but the file is still private business information.

## What the numbers mean

- **Pending** means recorded Issued or Received cheques.
- **Cleared** and **Bounced** are excluded from pending totals.
- **Overdue** uses the cheque date before your local today and the recorded pending status.
- Bounced exposure is shown separately.
- Amounts use your display currency; no exchange conversion is performed.
- Monthly activity uses cheque dates and current recorded statuses, not historical settlement events.

Reports are not bank balances, verified debts, proof of clearance, or financial advice.

## Before sharing a report

- Confirm the active business and current cheque statuses.
- Check that inward-cheque visibility matches the intended report scope.
- Review party and bank names.
- Store the downloaded file securely.
- Remove or correct accidental data before sharing outside the organisation.

## Export problems

If an export fails:

1. Confirm Premium access is active.
2. Check your connection and retry.
3. For a background job, refresh **Export jobs**.
4. Request a new export if the job is failed.
5. Do not share failed download links or private internal URLs.
