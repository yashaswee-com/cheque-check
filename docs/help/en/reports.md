# Review reports and exports

Reports help you understand what is happening in your business without manually checking each cheque one by one.

They give you a clearer picture of pending payments, completed work, account activity, and business performance over time.

## Why reports matter

Reports are useful when you want to:

- see what is still pending
- review cheque movement by date
- find overdue or urgent items
- understand activity across parties, accounts, or tags
- prepare business summaries and reviews

## What you can usually review

Depending on your workflow, reports may include:

- totals by status
- cheques due soon
- payment patterns by account
- party-wise summaries
- business-level review information
- filtered views by date or category

## How to use a report

1. Open the report or analytics area.
2. Choose the time period or view you need.
3. Apply filters such as party, account, status, or tag.
4. Review the results.
5. Export or save the result when needed.

## How to export data

If your plan supports it, you can export useful data for review, sharing, or record keeping. Exports can help with:

- monthly review
- managers or account review
- customer communication
- internal tracking and audits

## Best practices

- review reports often, not only when something is wrong
- look at status and outstanding amounts first
- filter by account or party when you want a clearer picture
- use reports to spot patterns, not just one-off problems

## Common mistakes to avoid

- ignoring overdue or pending totals
- exporting without checking the filters
- leaving old records in unclear states
- using reports without updating statuses first

## Quick summary

Reports turn a big mess of cheque details into a simple business picture. They help you see what is happening, what matters now, and what deserves attention.
