# Cheque Kaise Bhare: Simple English Guide

If you search **“cheque kaise bhare”**, meaning “how should I fill a cheque,” follow these fields in order: date, payee, amount in numbers, amount in words, crossing instructions, and signature.

This English guide is intentionally simple so a first-time cheque writer can complete a review checklist.

## Cheque kaise bhare — first, date

Write the cheque’s intended date clearly. Use a future date only when you deliberately intend to issue a post-dated cheque.

## Second, payee name

Write the name of the person or business that should receive the payment. This person is the payee. Double-check spelling.

## Third, amount in numbers

Write the amount in the box close to the printed currency symbol. Keep the figures tight so nothing can be added easily.

## Fourth, amount in words

Write the amount in words on the long line. It must match the number amount. Use “only” after the amount where suitable.

## Fifth, account-payee instruction

If an account-payee cheque is appropriate, use the crossing and wording accepted by the bank. Do not assume “A/C Payee” can be added in any arbitrary format to every instrument.

## Sixth, signature

Sign using the registered authorized signature. If the account needs joint signatures, make sure all required signatories complete it.

## Cheque filling example

```text
Date: 21/11/2026
Pay: Manish Garments
₹: 8,500/-
Rupees: Eight Thousand Five Hundred Only
Signature: Authorized account signature
```

## Before giving the cheque

Ask:

- Is the payee name correct?
- Do the figures and words match?
- Is the date right?
- Is the account funded or planned for the presentation?
- Is the cheque recorded with its number and status?
- Is only the required person signing?

Never leave a signed cheque blank and never share bank OTP, PIN, or password to “confirm” a cheque.
