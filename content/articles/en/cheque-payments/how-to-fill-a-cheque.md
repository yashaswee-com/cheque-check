# How to Fill a Cheque Correctly

To fill a cheque correctly, write the date, payee, amount, and purpose clearly, then sign with the signature registered on the account. Avoid blank spaces that can be altered.

## Step-by-step cheque filling

### 1. Write the date

Use the actual issue date or the intended cheque date. A future date creates a [post-dated cheque](/en/articles/pdc-cheque-meaning).

### 2. Enter the payee

Write the correct beneficiary after **Pay**. Check the [payee meaning](/en/articles/payee-meaning) and use the name expected by the recipient.

### 3. Write the amount in figures

Enter the amount close to the currency symbol in the figures box. Avoid leaving room for extra digits.

### 4. Write the amount in words

Write the same amount clearly in the line below. End the amount with “only” where appropriate. The word amount must agree with the figures.

### 5. Review the account-payee instruction

For a crossed, account-payee cheque, follow the bank’s format and the intended payment arrangement. Compare with a [bearer cheque](/en/articles/bearer-cheque) before choosing.

### 6. Sign

Sign as registered with the bank and verify that authorized signatories have completed any joint-signature requirement.

## Example

```text
Date: 21/11/2026
Pay: Pragati Traders
Amount in figures: ₹25,000/-
Amount in words: Rupees Twenty-Five Thousand Only
Account payee: As instructed
Signature: Authorized signature
```

## Final cheque checklist

- Payee spelling is correct.
- Date is intended and readable.
- Figures and words match.
- Blank spaces cannot easily be misused.
- Signature is registered.
- Relevant bank account has planned funds.
- The cheque number is recorded.
- A received copy or proof is retained where appropriate.

Never sign blank cheques. If ink corrections are needed, follow your bank’s correction and counter-signature requirements.
