# Cancelled Cheque Signature Required or Not?

**A signature on a cancelled cheque is not universally required.** Some organizations request an account-holder signature as an identity or verification step, while others only need clearly cancelled instrument details.

Always follow the requesting bank, employer, lender, or service provider’s written requirement.

## What a cancelled cheque proves

A cancelled cheque generally shows:

- account-holder name as printed by the bank;
- account number;
- bank and branch;
- IFSC and MICR details.

Crossing and writing “CANCELLED” makes the cheque unsuitable for ordinary payment. The signature itself does not create the account proof.

## When a signature may be requested

Signature may be requested when:

- the recipient has a verification policy requiring it;
- the cheque is attached to a signed mandate;
- the account holder must confirm a change request;
- a handwritten instruction accompanies the cheque.

If the form says “signature required,” ask the requester where the signature should appear. Do not sign in the normal payment-signature field unless instructed and necessary.

## Safer way to prepare a cancelled cheque

1. Write **CANCELLED** clearly across the face.
2. Draw parallel lines to avoid making an ordinary payment.
3. Keep the printed name, account number, bank, branch, IFSC, and MICR readable.
4. Leave the amount and date fields blank unless specifically requested.
5. Use the minimum signature required by the verified request.
6. Remove unnecessary cheque leaves and details from the copy.

## Important privacy point

A cancelled cheque contains banking information. Share it through the requesting organization’s verified channel, not an unknown WhatsApp number or email. Keep a copy only when your document-retention policy allows it.

## What not to do

- Do not leave “CANCELLED” small or erasable.
- Do not sign a blank cheque for convenience.
- Do not write an amount on a proof-only cheque.
- Do not share an unused or uncancelled cheque when a cancelled cheque was requested.
- Do not provide OTPs, passwords, or PINs with the document.

If the recipient refuses a signed or unsigned proof, ask for its written checklist rather than changing the cheque repeatedly.
