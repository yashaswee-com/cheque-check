# How to Deposit a Cheque in a Bank

To deposit a cheque, confirm that it is addressed to the correct account holder, check the date and endorsements, complete the bank’s deposit method, and keep the deposit reference until clearance.

Bank acceptance methods and settlement times vary. Use your bank’s current branch, mobile, or cheque-collection process.

## Before deposit

Check:

- the payee name;
- cheque date and stale-date risk;
- amount in figures and words;
- visible signature and instrument condition;
- any crossing or account-payee instruction;
- required endorsement or account-number details;
- where the cheque must be deposited.

If the cheque is post-dated, follow the applicable date rather than depositing early. See [PDC Cheque Meaning](/en/articles/pdc-cheque-meaning).

## Branch deposit workflow

1. Endorse the back only as required by your bank.
2. Complete the deposit slip with payee account, cheque number, drawer bank, and amount.
3. Place the cheque securely in the bank’s deposit envelope or hand it to the authorized desk.
4. Retain the stamped receipt, acknowledgement, or deposit reference.
5. Record the date and expected status update.

## Mobile or drop-box deposit

When permitted:

- use the bank’s official app or designated machine;
- capture all four cheque edges clearly;
- retain the physical cheque for the period required by the bank;
- mark and store it according to the instructions;
- watch for duplicate-deposit warnings or rejection messages.

Do not destroy or redeposit a cheque until the bank’s instructions allow it.

## After deposit

“Deposited” is not the same as “cleared.” Check:

- whether the cheque is sent for collection;
- the expected clearing update;
- any return memo or reason;
- whether funds are provisionally or finally credited.

## Received-cheque record

Save:

1. cheque number and date;
2. amount;
3. payer;
4. deposit date;
5. deposit reference;
6. destination account;
7. clearance or return status.

This record prevents a later search through separate slips and messages.

For direction-specific tracking, read [Issued vs Received Cheques](/en/articles/issued-vs-received-cheques).
