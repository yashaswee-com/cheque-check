# Payee Meaning on a Cheque

The **payee** is the person, business, or organization intended to receive payment from a cheque. The account holder who issues the cheque is the drawer or payer.

For example, if Manish Garments writes a cheque to Pragati Traders, Pragati Traders is the payee.

## Payee versus payer

| Term         | Meaning                                                               |
| ------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Payer/drawer | The person or business issuing the cheque                             |
| Payee        | The person or business receiving the cheque                           |
| Bank         | The institution holding the payer’s account and processing the cheque |

The payee name normally appears after the printed words **Pay** or **Pay to**.

## Payee name examples

- `Pay: Pragati Traders`
- `Pay: Asha Mehta`
- `Pay: Navrang Distributors`
- `Pay: Yourself` when moving money to the issuer’s own account, subject to the intended workflow

The correct name should match the intended beneficiary and bank acceptance requirements. Avoid unexplained abbreviations when the legal or account name matters.

## Why the correct payee matters

A wrong or unclear payee can lead to:

- delayed presentation;
- bank rejection;
- return of the cheque;
- reconciliation confusion;
- an extra replacement cheque.

If you identify a mistake before handing over the cheque, follow your bank’s guidance. Do not make unnecessary or misleading corrections.

## Account payee versus bearer

An “account payee” instruction directs payment into the named payee’s account through the crossed-cheque workflow. A bearer cheque can generally be treated differently depending on the printed format and bank handling. Read [Bearer Cheque](/en/articles/bearer-cheque) for the comparison.

## Recording the payee correctly

For business records, keep:

- the payee or supplier name;
- cheque number;
- date;
- amount;
- bank account;
- business unit;
- expected presentation or receipt date;
- current status.

Do not confuse the payee with the bank or use a generic label such as “supplier” when multiple suppliers share the same cheque list.
